# Delete your SpecWithCode account

This page is for the SpecWithCode Connect account you sign in with in the SpecWithCode apps, including SpecWithCode for Android and iPhone. SpecWithCode is made by Anton Abyzov.

## How to request deletion

1. Email [anton.abyzov@gmail.com](mailto:anton.abyzov@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20SpecWithCode%20account) from the email address you sign in with.
2. Use the subject "Delete my SpecWithCode account".
3. We confirm by email when the account is deleted, within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your SpecWithCode Connect account at Clerk, our sign-in provider: your email address, your name and the sign-in methods you linked (for example Google or GitHub).
- The relay's records for your account: the computers linked to it and the phones registered to it.

## What is kept

We keep nothing for your account after it is deleted. Your projects, chats and settings never reach us. They stay on your own computers until you delete them there. To remove the app's data from a phone, sign out and uninstall the app.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for details.
