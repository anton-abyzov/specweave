Opus 5.5 in Claude Code
Model, effort, per thread
claude.ai/code
Opus 5.5 is the default model in Claude Code.
One click to switch. It's ticked at the top.
Fable 5.1, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 4.5 sit right below. Keys 1 to 4.
Then effort: Faster on the left, Smarter on the right.

Turn it up for planning and reviews. Keep Medium for routine work.

claude.ai/code
Watch what the coordinator does with a message.
A question about a match page gets its own thread, with its own scope.
It even splits a thread: the TikTok question leaves the tracking thread and gets its own look.
claude.ai/code
Open a thread and it's a full agent session.
A live checklist: read memory, audit, sweep 224 checkouts, push the index.
Threads get messages from the coordinator too. This one got 7.
And then the wall every heavy user hits: usage limit reached.
claude.ai/code
So here's what I asked the coordinator.
"Make every thread ready for a handoff, so I can continue on another subscription."
It started a Handoff readiness thread for exactly that. One ask, one thread.

Chapter 5
Routines, limits and results
What keeps a project moving
claude.ai/code
Routines are agents on a schedule.
A morning briefing, email triage, a system health check.
PR digests, dependency checks, release notes when a PR merges.
claude.ai/code
When the weekly limit hits, the whole project pauses.
Claude says it will pick things up at 10:00 AM by itself.
Auto-continue restarts every thread when the limit resets.
Nice. But that can be days away. What if you can't wait?

SpecWeave keeps the work itself in git: spec, tasks, claims, next step.
Say "hand off" or let it fire at 90% of a limit. It pushes state and even uncommitted edits.
In Codex, Cursor, another account or machine: say "pick up".
Same spec, same task, same next step and acceptance criteria. Nothing lost.
Open source and free: npm i -g specweave
