Start SpecWithCode with a project,
a coordinator and two workers.
This run uses SpecWithCode 1.0.45.
Settings, General, About
shows the version on your Mac.
Click the plus next to Projects.
New project starts one from a name. Local
folder adopts a repo you already have.
Here it's greet-app, with SpecWeave
and one increment of two tasks.
The project opens on its coordinator.
Choose Full access for this demo.
It applies to every chat in this project.
Then ask the coordinator
for a two-part change:
one worker per task, with
titles and a short report.
The coordinator does
none of the work itself.
It starts two worker threads,
and they appear in the
right panel under Working.
Each one gets a brief: claim,
implement, test, close and report.
The workers share one checkout,
so each works on its own files.
Each plan chip counts the
increment's tasks done,
from zero to two of two.
The whole run took under a
minute; it's sped up here.
Open a worker. Its result
stays in its own thread.
Its first message is the
coordinator's brief, with your words.
A final message with three headed
sections becomes a report card,
saved in the increment's reports folder.
Open report reads it in full.
Click the plan chip: both tasks are done,
and both acceptance criteria are met.
Open spec and Open
increment go to its files.
This chip matched increment
0001 by the thread's title,
so put the increment number
in each worker's title.
