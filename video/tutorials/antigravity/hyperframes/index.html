Antigravity in SpecWithCode: plan, build, review.
In your repo, run specweave init with the Antigravity adapter.
It adds AGENTS.md and eleven SpecWeave skills.
Open SpecWithCode, then Settings, Providers.
Click Add provider and pick Antigravity.
SpecWithCode installs its own Antigravity agent.
Click Sign in and finish with your Google account in the browser.
This one is already signed in.
Add the repo: the plus next to Projects, Local folder, then its path.
A new chat opens. Pick Gemini 3.8 Flash under Antigravity,
and set Full access for this demo.
Type a dollar sign, pick sw-increment, and describe the change.
Antigravity writes spec.md:
acceptance criteria, and a task with its files and test.
Then send sw-do. Antigravity claims the task, changes the code,
and runs the test. The task is marked done only when the test passes.
Click Open diff to review the code, the test,
and the ledger line that records who finished the task.
Next, sw-done verifies and closes the increment.
